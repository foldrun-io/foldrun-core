# Plain English

1. Lead with the fact, not the setup. "0.2mm per tip" beats "the RG-100 features".
2. One idea per sentence. If a sentence has an "and" holding two ideas together, cut it in half.
3. Prefer the concrete noun: *bucket*, *tip*, *millimetre* — not *mechanism*, *increment*, *measurement solution*.
4. Never hedge a number. Either you know it or you leave it out.
